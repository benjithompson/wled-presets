# Domain glossary

The words the code and its reviews use for this project. Add a term when a module is named after it.

- **Yard**: the house and garden with lights on it, as the layout describes them.
- **Fixture** (an *object* in the editor): one thing in the layout with LEDs on it: the roof, a line, a bush, a tree, a semicircle or a custom object. Its *kind* says which.
- **Stage**: the layout turned into LED positions, ready for effects and games to draw on ([shared/layout.ts](shared/layout.ts) `buildStage`).
- **Line**: the queue visitors join to use the lights. Whoever's turn it is holds the lights ([server/src/line.ts](server/src/line.ts)).
- **Game**: something one or two visitors play on the lights from their phone. The catalog lists them ([shared/games.ts](shared/games.ts)), and the game host runs one at a time ([server/src/game/host.ts](server/src/game/host.ts)).
- **Round**: what every game shares around its own rules: its phase and how long it has been in it, starting again after the end, its scoreboard, and the status when the layout has nowhere to play it ([server/src/game/round.ts](server/src/game/round.ts)). Phases the host goes by: `ready` (waiting, with the scoreboard) and `over` or `won` (ended).
- **Show**: the light show the whole yard puts on around a game: a mood, pulses, fireworks, sweeps ([server/src/game/show.ts](server/src/game/show.ts)). A game draws it with its other effects through `Fx`.
- **Scoreboard** (a *board*): the top scores for one game, or for one level of a game, with three-letter initials.
- **Tones**: the colors games play with (orbs, beads, buttons, layers), far apart on real LEDs and none of the colors that mean something else: white, gold (points) and red (a mistake) ([server/src/game/kit.ts](server/src/game/kit.ts) `TONES`, next to `COLORS`). Colors themselves, and the small maths around them, are in [shared/color.ts](shared/color.ts).
- **Switched off**: a device an admin turned off here. Off holds: scenes, colors and games leave it dark, and only an editor's preview wakes it while the admin looks. `takesFrames` ([server/src/lightSender.ts](server/src/lightSender.ts)) is the one rule for which devices get frames; the switches themselves are in `Lights`.
- **Transition**: how one show of the lights gives way to the next: a crossfade, a wipe, a flash and more, or "random" (a different one each time) ([shared/transitions.ts](shared/transitions.ts)). Clips in a sequence come in and go out with one; scenes someone picks change with the one in the settings (the auto-revert changes at once).
- **Showcase**: a 60-second sequence to a beat that the sequence editor builds for whatever yard the layout has, with its music. One score drives both: the objects are the band (the trees the kick, the bushes the hi-hats and bass, the roof's segments the melody's keys), and shapes (sunrises, objects of your own design) clap with the roof, and whole objects blink one after another (the snare roll, the hook on the trees, chases in the second drop); every quarter note moves (a *beat look*: a light that walks, each object wiping side to side or up and down, a laser, a wave across the yard, a shockwave from its middle, a chase of whole objects), lighting more of the yard as the music grows, on dark. The big moves target the whole yard, so objects added later join in; 3-D pictures play only where the music swells ([shared/showcase.ts](shared/showcase.ts); the music is synthesized in the browser, [web/src/pages/admin/sequence/showcaseAudio.ts](web/src/pages/admin/sequence/showcaseAudio.ts)).
- **Sequence file**: a sequence saved to a file (`.wledseq`, JSON) with the audio it plays inside, to keep it or open it on another copy of the site ([shared/sequenceFile.ts](shared/sequenceFile.ts); the browser's side, [web/src/pages/admin/sequence/sequenceFiles.ts](web/src/pages/admin/sequence/sequenceFiles.ts)). It opens as a new, unsaved sequence.
- **String fit**: the light string an object is made of limits its drawing: its LEDs sit a fixed distance apart along the wire, so the wire cannot be drawn longer than the string, and a shape's winding (a tree's turns, strands or passes, a semicircle's rows, a bush's spiral or ring) is worked out from it ([shared/stringFit.ts](shared/stringFit.ts); the editor applies it with every edit, next to keeping locked objects in place).
- **Front view angle**: where the front view looks at the yard from, to match a picture of the house taken from off to one side, from above or from close by. The yard turns under the picture; effects and games do not change ([web/src/yard/frontCamera.ts](web/src/yard/frontCamera.ts), `Layout.frontView`).
- **String** (a *strand*): one length of lights as it comes, perStrand LEDs of its light type. An object is made of whole strings, one after the other in the order of the device's LED numbers; where each one starts can be set by hand (`Fixture.stringBreaks`, [shared/lights.ts](shared/lights.ts) `stringRanges`).
- **Roofline scale**: the yard's scale (layout units per meter) worked out from the roof: from one corner's LED to the next is so many LEDs of known spacing, so the roofline as drawn over the picture, over the string along it, is the scale. It is kept with every edit unless set by hand (`Layout.scaleBy`); objects keep the size they are drawn at ([shared/yardScale.ts](shared/yardScale.ts)).
- **Mark** (Shape to the lights): an LED seen on a tree, with where it is on it (degrees round from the front, and how high) and how far it is moved off the shape by hand (`offset`). The tree's spiral is bent to go through its marks; a wave is made of rows between its turns, each as wide as its LEDs (`waveRows`) ([shared/layout.ts](shared/layout.ts) `Fixture.marks`, `markedPlaces`; the wizard, [web/src/pages/admin/shapeWizard.tsx](web/src/pages/admin/shapeWizard.tsx)).
- **Pick**: what a click, a tap or a hold in a 2D view of the editor selects: the object whose wire or LEDs pass nearest the pointer, else the smallest one whose outline it is inside, whatever is drawn on top ([web/src/yard/pick.ts](web/src/yard/pick.ts)). On a touch screen a finger held still for a second picks; a tap does not, and one finger pans. A picked object is framed: centered and filling the views.
- **WLED settings**: a device's own settings, as WLED keeps them (/json/cfg): its LED outputs, brightness limiter, power-up state, real-time and sync, names, Wi-Fi radio. The console edits a copy and sends the device only what changed; Wi-Fi, the access point and the update lock are never sent ([shared/wledConfig.ts](shared/wledConfig.ts); the dialog, [web/src/pages/admin/WledSettings.tsx](web/src/pages/admin/WledSettings.tsx)).
